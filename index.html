Welcome to Film Finder

Film Finder is a movie discovery and ticket booking platform. Browse what is showing, find a movie that suits your mood, and book your seats in a few simple steps.

Our Purpose

  • Entertainment: We help people pick a great movie for a night out or a night in.
  • Variety: Our line-up covers action, comedy, animation, drama, romance and horror.
  • Accessibility: Everything is clearly labelled and works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Convenience: You can browse, save a watchlist and book tickets without leaving the site.

Who Is It For?

  • Friends: Pick a funny or action-packed movie and book seats together.
  • Families: Find family-friendly movies and book child tickets for the little ones.
  • Students: Add movies to a watchlist and book student tickets when you are ready.
  • Other: Couples on date night and solo movie fans are welcome too.

What we offer

  • Movie discovery - read about each movie, see session times and ticket prices.
  • Personalised recommendations - answer three quick questions and we will find the perfect movie for you!
  • Book tickets now! - choose your movie, session and payment method.
